Questions

Good to know

Is this a paid job?

Yes. You are employed by the program's company in Rwanda and receive a monthly salary during your time in the program. The planned salary is about RWF 140,000 a month in Year 1, rising to about RWF 163,000 in Year 2 and RWF 186,000 in Year 3. Final terms will be in your contract.

How are apprentices selected?

Through the 3-day AI Startup Bootcamp. We look at how you think, learn and work with others — not only at your CV.

Can I stay after 3 years?

The standard path is to graduate. A small number of graduates may be invited to stay as leaders whose job is to help the next group launch their businesses.

Do I have to start a business?

Going independent is the main goal, and we support it strongly. If you choose a job instead, we help you find one with your experience and a reference letter.

What happens each year?

Your contract is renewed once a year. We review your progress against goals written in your contract from day one. If the goals are not met, the program ends at that point.
